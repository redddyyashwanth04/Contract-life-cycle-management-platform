EMPLOYMENT OFFER LETTER

Dear Candidate,
Nimbus Labs is pleased to offer you the position of Senior Engineer on the terms below.

1. COMPENSATION
Your base salary will be Rs. 24,00,000 per annum, payable monthly. You will be eligible for a performance bonus of up to 15% of base salary.

2. PROBATION
You will be on probation for a period of six (6) months, during which your employment may be terminated upon seven (7) days' notice.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
You agree that all work product, inventions and developments created during your employment shall be the sole property of Nimbus Labs, and you hereby assign all right, title and interest therein to the company.

4. NON-COMPETITION
For a period of twenty-four (24) months following termination of your employment, you shall not work for any business that competes with Nimbus Labs, anywhere in the world.

5. TERMINATION
After the probation period, either party may terminate this employment upon fifteen (15) days' written notice.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This offer is governed by the laws of the State of Telangana, India.
